# Release 1.52.0

Policy `oncoref.target-selection.2026-11-26.1` · Comparison panel `oncoref.human-grch38-integrated-tpm.2026-11-26.1` · Sequence-role policy `oncoref.target-selection-sequence-roles.2026-11-21.1` · Species policy `oncoref.species-screening-policy.2026-11-21.1` · Canine comparison panel `oncoref.dog-canfam31-tpm.2026-11-26.1` · Murine comparison panel `oncoref.mouse-grcm39-tpm.2026-11-26.1`.

OncoRef `1.52.0` unifies `CTA-E` and `CTA-P` into a mutually exclusive 4-way host-physiology partition (`{CTA-M, CTA-F, CTA-MF, CTA-P}` / `{RA-M, RA-F, RA-MF, RA-P}`), rescues adult female germ-cell and reproductive-age expression in humans via `GTEx v11 AGE 20–49` stratification plus adult human folliculogenesis/oocyte RNA-seq (`GSE107746`), harmonizes bulk somatic genomic-DNA library QC (`<= 2.5%` ectopic `OR`/`Olfr`/`COR >= 5 TPM`) and phase-invariant `step = 1` 31-mer + `ncRNA` paralog guards across species, and delivers the first genome-wide quantitative canine (`Canis lupus familiaris`, Ensembl 98 `CanFam3.1`) antigen catalog alongside complete three-species (`human`–`dog`–`mouse`) ortholog concordance:

- **Human (`Homo sapiens`, `9606`, `19,893` PEGs / `20,099` Ensembl 115 coding loci):** **325 CTA** (`224 CTA-M`, `13 CTA-F` [`+62.5%`], `40 CTA-MF`, `48 CTA-P`), **95 Cancer Antigen only**, **143 Reproductive Antigen only** (`468` total `RA`; `563` candidate union `CA ∪ RA`), **1,081 Other**, and **18,249 Unsafe**.
- **Dog (`Canis lupus familiaris`, `9615`, `20,082` PEGs / `20,199` Ensembl 98 `CanFam3.1` coding loci, `reference-dog-20261126`):** **54 CTA** (`42 CTA-M`, `0 CTA-F` [fail-closed on unmeasured male canine `resting_mammary`], `8 CTA-MF`, `4 CTA-P`), **59 Cancer Antigen only**, **421 Reproductive Antigen only** (`397 RA-M-only`, `6 RA-F-only`, `14 RA-MF-only`, `4 RA-P-only`; `475` total `RA`; `534` candidate union), **2,573 Other**, and **16,975 Unsafe** across **8** canine tumor cohorts (`7` spontaneous companion-dog patient cohorts + `CLBL-1` `DLBCL`; `571` samples).
- **Mouse (`Mus musculus`, `10090`, `21,433` PEGs / `21,760` Ensembl 115 `GRCm39` coding loci, `reference-mouse-20261126`):** **12 CTA** (`5 CTA-M`, `3 CTA-F`, `2 CTA-MF`, `2 CTA-P`), **24 Cancer Antigen only**, **305 Reproductive Antigen only** (`155 RA-M-only`, `26 RA-F-only`, `49 RA-MF-only`, `75 RA-P-only`), **2,541 Other**, and **18,551 Unsafe**.

## Highlights

1. **Unified 4-way physiological sex-restriction partition (`CTA-P` / `RA-P` replacing `CTA-E` / `RA-E` + overlay):**
   - Folds embryogenesis (`embryo`), placentation (`placenta`), and postpartum lactation (`lactating_breast` / `lactating_mammary`) into a single disjoint 4th host-physiology slot **`Pregnancy / peripartum-only` (`RA-P-only` / `CTA-P`)**—antigens active in the pregnancy/lactation continuum and silent (`< 8` TPM/nTPM; `< 16` with `not_detected` IHC in human) across every adult male and non-pregnant, non-lactating adult female organ (`48` human `CTA-P`, `4` canine `CTA-P`, `2` murine `CTA-P`).
   - Preserves `CTA-E`, `RA-E`, `ERA`, `PRA`, `MRA`, `FRA`, and `SRA` as backward-compatible selector aliases in `antigen_sets.SEX_SETS`, while `HostContext(pregnant=True)` / `HostContext(lactating=True)` and `species_target_selection.peripartum_antigen_ids()` enforce organ-level peripartum exclusions on the `12` human CTAs (`13` RAs; out of `16` total peripartum CTAs / `19` RAs) that also express in adult testis (`PLAC1`, `XAGE3`, `MAGEA4/8/10`) or resting female breast (`LALBA`, `CSN2`).

2. **Reproductive-age female GTEx stratification (`ages 20–49`) & adult human folliculogenesis/oocyte RNA-seq (`GSE107746`):**
   - Packages GTEx v11 public donor age decades (`oncoref/data/gtex-donor-age.csv`, `gtex.donor_age()`) to stratify `XX` female reproductive and resting-mammary `HostContext` exposures to reproductive-age donors (`20–49`, avoiding ~60% postmenopausal ovarian cortex dilution across ages `50–79`).
   - Integrates stage-replicated adult human folliculogenesis RNA-seq (`GSE107746`, Zhang et al. 2018 *Mol Cell*, PMID:30420494; `128` QC-passing single-cell/follicle libraries across primordial, primary, secondary, and antral oocytes plus primordial-to-preovulatory granulosa cells, `oncoref.reproductive_age_female`, with 1:1 pre-2020 HGNC synonym recovery including `H1-8`/`H1FOO` and `PRR23E`/`C3orf56`).
   - Promotes **`CPSF4L` (`265.7 TPM` adult oocytes, male `<= 3.69 TPM`) → `CTA-F`** and **`MOS` (`734.1 TPM` adult antral oocytes) → `CTA-MF`** (`323 → 325` human CTAs), expands `CTA-F` from `8` to **`13`** (`CSN2`, `LALBA`, `DPPA3`, `KHDC3L`, `ZAR1L`, `ZP4`, `VRTN`, `PRR23E`, `CPSF4L`, `IZUMO1R`, `ACCSL`, `OR1N1`, `TBXT`), moves **`BMP15` (`369.5 TPM` oocytes)** and **`OR52D1`** into `RA-F-only`, and reclassifies `13` dual-sex germline CTAs (`DMRTC2`, `CIMIP2A`, `CBY2`, `EBLN1`, `LYPD4`, `DCAF4L2`, `MAGEC1`, `FTHL17`, `MAGEB16`, `CCDC42`, `ANKRD60`, `SPO11`, `TGM4`) from `CTA-M` to `CTA-MF`, while preserving exact `19,893 / 19,893` `2026-11-20.2` baseline and single-rule ablation parity (`include_reproductive_age_female=False`).

3. **Genome-wide quantitative canine (`Canis lupus familiaris`, Taxon `9615`) antigen catalog (`oncoref.dog-canfam31-tpm.2026-11-26.1`):**
   - Evaluates all **20,082** protein-equivalence groups (**20,199** Ensembl 98 `CanFam3.1` coding loci, **45,036** sequences, cross-referenced to NCBI `RefSeq 106` `UU_Cfam_GSD_1.0`) across **25** `Bgee 15.2` experiments + **300** sex-stratified `GSE219045` normal/reproductive libraries + **64** `GSE119810` normal female mammary biopsies (`24` required / `36` evaluated adult somatic contexts; `13` RNA-measured + `1` `proteome_only` reproductive compartments; fail-closed on unmeasured male canine `resting_mammary`), **Pfizer 38-tissue TMT proteomics** (`MSV000099744` with `10%` within-plex reproductive bleed subtraction and `>= 80%` overlap-coefficient matching), and **8** canine tumor cohorts (`7` spontaneous patient cohorts—`OSA_NCI`, `OSA_COTC030`, `OSA_CCOGC`, `CMT`, `HSA`, `LYM`, `GLIOMA`—plus `CLBL-1` `DLBCL`; `571` samples), guarding Cufflinks FPKM cohorts via phase-invariant `step = 1` 31-mer + `ncRNA` and intronless `Unsafe` 9-mer retrocopy filters.
   - Resolves all **14** literature-curated canine RT-PCR nominations (`hindriks2025`, `nemec2019`): validates **`PRAME` (`CTA-M`)** as a quantitative canine CTA (`110.7 TPM` lymphoma, `100.8 TPM` testis, `0.97 TPM` somatic baseline), places `MAGEB10` and `SSX6` in `Reproductive Antigen only`, keeps `MAGEA13P` (`Other:Expression`) and ambiguous `MAGE` (`Other:Expression / Reproductive Antigen only`) below `CA`, keeps `CAGE1`/`CTCFL`/`DDX53` in `Other:Review`, and excludes somatic-expressed `AKR1E2`, `IGF2BP3`, `NT5C1B` (`ENSCAFG00000003760`), `PAGE3-like`, `SPECC1`, and `TPX2` as `Unsafe` (`1 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 3 + 6 = 14`).

4. **Harmonized murine `<= 2.5%` ectopic `Olfr` gDNA bulk QC & complete three-species CTA ortholog concordance:**
   - Applies the `<= 2.5%` ectopic olfactory-receptor `>= 5 TPM` genomic-DNA ceiling and phase-invariant `step = 1` 31-mer + `ncRNA` paralog checks uniformly to murine `GRCm39` (`oncoref.mouse-grcm39-tpm.2026-11-26.1`, `305` `RA only`, `24` `CA only`, `12` `CTA`).
   - Publishes complete 100%-coverage `human`–`dog`–`mouse` CTA ortholog concordance (`docs/antigen-whitepaper/cross-species-orthologs.csv` and [multi-species whitepaper](species-antigen-whitepaper.md#cross-species-cta-orthology-across-human-dog-and-mouse)).
