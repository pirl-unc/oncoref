# Releases and Archive

OncoRef pins every dataset, classification rule, and output artifact by SHA-256 digest so analyses remain reproducible across package versions.

## Recent releases

- [Release 1.52.0](cta-release-1.52.0.md) — Unified 4-way physiological sex-restriction partition (`{CTA-M, CTA-F, CTA-MF, CTA-P}` / `{RA-M, RA-F, RA-MF, RA-P}`), reproductive-age female GTEx (`20–49`) + adult human folliculogenesis/oocyte RNA-seq (`GSE107746`, `325` human CTAs with `13` `CTA-F`), genome-wide quantitative Ensembl 98 `CanFam3.1` canine antigen catalog (`20,082` PEGs, `54` CTAs across `8` canine tumor cohorts, `reference-dog-20261126`), harmonized murine `<= 2.5%` ectopic `Olfr` gDNA bulk QC (`reference-mouse-20261126`), and complete `human`–`dog`–`mouse` CTA ortholog concordance.
- [Release 1.51.0](cta-release-1.51.0.md) — Holistic 52-shard GTEx v11 deconvolution & cross-loader `(qc, cleanup, normalize)` harmonization (#764, #778, #780), promoted multi-assay safety/eligibility reconciliations (#765–#769, #775, #779), potency-proven clinical `TCR-T` / `ImmTAC` toxicity gold-standard benchmark (#784), symmetric `RA-*` / `CTA-*` + `CTA-P` sex-restriction rules with `LALBA` & 159-gene female-lineage interrogation (#776, #784), and deep human/murine `CTA` & `RA` audit tightening tumor OR genomic-DNA library QC (`<= 2.5%`), promoting `KRTAP20-4 -> CTA-M`, resolving `TSSK2 -> RA-M-only`, and gating murine sorted/activated `ImmGen`/BMDM immune subsets while completing 16-compartment GEO zero-count reproductive coverage (`323` human CTAs; `12` murine CTAs).
- [Release 1.50.0](cta-release-1.50.0.md) — Decoupled historical audit manifest SHA-256 checks from live runtime modules (#753), unified runtime species selection across human, mouse, and dog without `taxon_id == 9606` forks (#754), consolidated caches onto `datacache` + `OPENVAX_DATA_CACHE` with offline `allow_download=False` / `preflight` checks and `pandas 3.1+` compatibility (#747, #761, #762), unified genome-wide human catalog evaluation onto `evaluate_species_catalog` (`19,893 / 19,893` exact parity) with integrated v1.49 evidence previews (#755), and quarantined 22 frozen legacy `cta*.py` modules under `oncoref._legacy` while modularizing `oncoref.expression` (#756).
- [Release 1.49.0](cta-release-1.49.0.md) — Unified `reference_data` externalization of ~51 MB of archival/calibration tables cutting the PyPI wheel to ~15.9 MB (#738), HPA v23 normal-library & Ensembl 115 rare-cell attribution adjudications (#711, #712), `THYMCA` WHO thymic carcinoma cohort & linear GRCh38.p13 TPM `GSE125285` `BCC`/`cSCC` upgrades (#737), quantitative adult human normal panels across unmeasured home tissues (#735), and genome-wide Ensembl 115 `GRCm39` murine antigen catalog ingestion (#734).
- [Release 1.48.0](cta-release-1.48.0.md) — Controlled-access local FASTQ + Salmon builder and `unc-nutm1` provenance scaffolding (#665, #604), `TCGA-TGCT` `TCGA-2G-AAFL-01` parenchymal admixture vs germ-cell localization audit (#713), umbrella expression coverage, proxy-scale cohort disclosures & package-data retention audit (#737, #738), and germline coding polymorphism subtraction, isoform-selectivity evaluation & ncORF/TE screening contracts (#736).
- [Release 1.47.0](cta-release-1.47.0.md) — Multi-species Ensembl ID & schema helpers and audited partial technical-compartment matrices (#732, #733, #737), structured legacy `oncoref.cta_*` deprecations and `Disposable`/`Silent` reconciliation (#647, #730), offline `sequence_bundle` ↔ `PeptideIndex`/`antigen_sets` bridge and near-self subtraction contract (#640, #731), and hard-link-aware bounded cache inventory & pruning (#591).
- [Release 1.46.0](cta-release-1.46.0.md) — `target_selection`-backed `sequence_bundle` (`oncoref-sequences-1.46.0.zip`, #725), `gene_ids` `'nan'` / `SCN11A` `'NaN'` synonym fix and `rna-protein-hpa-priors` cleanup (#727), complete `data_manifest` registration and multi-species `antigen_sets` API consistency (#727), canonical `target_selection` CTA wiring across `coverage`, `plots`, and `peptides` (#728), and full `pirlygenes` plotting/coverage parity plus clarified CTA curation & active-policy gate exports (#607, #729).
