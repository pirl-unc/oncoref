# Release 1.51.0

Policy `oncoref.target-selection.2026-11-25.1` · Comparison panel `oncoref.human-grch38-integrated-tpm.2026-11-25.1` · Sequence-role policy `oncoref.target-selection-sequence-roles.2026-11-21.1` · Species policy `oncoref.species-screening-policy.2026-11-21.1` · Murine comparison panel `oncoref.mouse-grcm39-tpm.2026-11-25.1`.

Across **19,893** human Ensembl 115 protein-equivalence groups (**20,099** coding loci), OncoRef `1.51.0` promotes holistic bulk/single-cell QC harmonization, multi-assay edge-case adjudications, symmetric `RA-*` / `CTA-*` sex-restriction rules, a potency-proven clinical `TCR-T` / `ImmTAC` toxicity gold-standard benchmark, and a genome-wide audit of human and murine `CTA` and `RA` catalogs—classifying **323 CTA** (`236 CTA-M`, `52 CTA-E`, `8 CTA-F`, `27 CTA-MF`; `16` non-exclusive `CTA-P`), **97 Cancer Antigen only**, **142 Reproductive Antigen only** (`465` total `RA`; `562` candidate union `CA ∪ RA`), **1,082 Other**, and **18,249 Unsafe** in human, alongside **12 CTA** (`5 CTA-M`, `2 CTA-E`, `3 CTA-F`, `2 CTA-MF`), **22 Cancer Antigen only**, and **261 Reproductive Antigen only** across **21,433** Ensembl 115 `GRCm39` murine protein groups (`reference-mouse-20261125`).

## Highlights

1. **Holistic GTEx v11 52-shard deconvolution & cross-loader `(qc, cleanup, normalize)` harmonization (`#764`, `#778`, `#780`):**
   - Modularizes `oncoref.gtex` (`loaders`, `deconvolution`, `plots`, `spec`) and extracts shared `oncoref.bulk_qc` primitives across GTEx v11, HPA v23 bulk & single-cell, per-sample tumor cohorts (`source_matrices`, `library_qc`), and multi-species `ExpressionDataset` readers (`bgee`, `barkbase`).
   - Separates patterned-flowcell index hopping (`PRM1`/`PRM2`, `KLK3`/`MSMB`/`ACP3`), droplet ambient milk/exocrine soup (`CSN2` in `HPA` salivary scRNA-seq), `<= 100 kb` `4q13.3` SCPP cis-chromatin spillover, and `XY` male cadaver galactorrhea (`8/336` GTEx `Breast_Mammary_Tissue` donors) from true parenchymal expression, with publication-ready figures in [GTEx reference & deconvolution](gtex.md).

2. **Multi-assay safety & eligibility reconciliations promoted to active policy (`#765`–`#769`, `#775`, `#779`):**
   - Promotes nuclear `snRNA-seq` pre-mRNA spillover clearance (`PLAC1`, `ZAN → CTA`; `MAGEA12` kept `Unsafe`, `#765`), isoform- and epitope-scoped `lete-cel` clinical pMHC clearance (`CTAG2` `ENST00000369585 → CTA`, `#766`), closed homologous repeat-family equivalence (`DUX4` + `20` `DUX4L*` repeat units `→ CTA-E`, `#767`), assay-boundary reconciliations (`MAGEB6`, `DKKL1`, `FOXR2`, `SPATA31A3`, `PPP4R3C`, `CSN2 → CTA`; `11` Note-`R` `TCGA-TGCT` spermatid markers `→ RA only`, `#768`), and 1-mismatch Hamming near-self peptide metrics (`#769`), while preserving exact reproducibility of the frozen `2026-11-20.2` baseline and orthogonal single-rule ablations via `human.evaluate_integrated_catalog(include_*=...)`.

3. **Potency-proven clinical `TCR-T` & `ImmTAC` toxicity gold standard (`#784`):**
   - Curates **16** endogenous-protein clinical `TCR-T` and soluble `ImmTAC` regimens (`oncoref.tcr_trials.gold_standard`, `evaluate_against_gold_standard`, `oncoref tcr-trials --table benchmark`), requiring objective tumor responses (`ORR > 0%`) to validate *in vivo* cytolytic potency.
   - Achieves **100% (`6/6`)** sensitivity to human clinical specificity toxicities (`CEACAM5` pan-colitis, `MLANA`/`PMEL`/`TYR` ocular/inner-ear melanocyte destruction, `MAGEA3 → MAGEA12/EPS8L2` fatal leukoencephalopathy, `MAGEA3 → TTN` fatal cardiogenic shock) and **100% (`5/5`)** recall on clinically proven safe CTAs (`NY-ESO-1`, `MAGEA4`, `PRAME`, `CTAG2`, `MAGEA10`), while segregating `0% ORR` (`IMA202` `MAGEA1`) and unreadout (`CT83`) trials.

4. **Symmetric `RA-*` / `CTA-*` sex-restriction rules, `CTA-P` / `RA-P` peripartum selector, and `LALBA` / female-lineage interrogation (`#776`, `#784`):**
   - Harmonizes `HostContext` organ protection with Stage 1 `Protected` (`8 TPM / 8 nTPM`) + negative-IHC (`2× = 16` in human compartments with `not_detected` IHC) rules (`LALBA` & `CSN2 → CTA-F`, `BTN1A1 → CTA-E` + `CTA-P`), introduces symmetric `RA-{M,E,F,MF}` / `RA-{M,E,F,MF}-only` selectors alongside `CTA-{M,E,F,MF}` and non-exclusive peripartum `CTA-P` / `RA-P` (`16` human CTAs, `19` human RAs), and publishes a 159-gene mammary/oocyte/meiotic/Müllerian/placental audit explaining the `29.5 : 1` `CTA-M` to `CTA-F` ratio.

5. **Deep human & murine `CTA` / `RA` catalog audit (`2026-11-25.1`):**
   - **Tumor genomic-DNA background QC (`library_qc.BACKGROUND_MAX_SHARE = 0.025`):** Tightens the per-sample olfactory-receptor `>= 5 TPM` ceiling from `20%` to **`2.5%`**, newly excluding **92** baseline-admitted gDNA-contaminated tumor libraries (`NEC_MERKEL: 68`, `SARC_PEC: 10`, `BL: 8`, `MCL: 2`, `HL: 1`, `B_ALL: 1`, `LAML: 1`, `T_ALL: 1`, tracked in `tumor-gdna-background-deltas.{csv,json}`) and demoting `22` single-cohort gDNA-background `CA only` proteins (`17` `OR*`s, `KRTAP9-7`, `C19orf85`, `SCYGR8`, `SEBOX`, `VHLL → Other:Expression`) plus `10` single-cohort male-reproductive proteins (`CAPZA3`, `DNAJB8`, `IQCF2`, `IQCF6`, `MAGEB18`, `RNASE9`, `TMEM225`, `SPATA31A6`, `SMIM42`, `ZCCHC13 → RA-M-only`).
   - **Human `#735` hair-follicle skin-bound & GTEx-v11 female-tract backfill:** Clears hair-follicle loci silent across 8 scalp scRNA donors and bounded near-zero across `1,405` GTEx hair-bearing leg/suprapubic skin biopsies (`P99 <= 0.50 TPM`, `max < 4.0 TPM`), promoting **`KRTAP20-4` (`50.4 TPM` across `8` clean `NEC_LUNG_LARGECELL` witnesses) to `CTA-M`** (`KRTAP20-3`, `KRTAP21-3`, `KRTAP6-2 → CA only`, `33` home-gated proteins in `CA only` and `4` in `CTA`), and backfills GTEx-v11-missing `vagina`/`uterus` measurements from pinned `rna_tissue_gtex:v23` + `hpa_tissue_samples:v23` (`TSSK2 → RA-M-only`, leaving `0` human CTAs/RAs unresolved for missing female-tract coverage).
   - **Murine sorted/activated immune gates & 16-compartment zero-count completion (`oncoref.mouse-grcm39-tpm.2026-11-25.1`):** Integrates `ImmGen` ULI (`GSE109125`), `ImmGen` MNP (`GSE122108`), and WT `LPS`/`IL-4` BMDMs (`GSE181223`) via finite-sample replicated `min(P95, X_{(n-1)})` gates—reclassifying inducible cytokines and myeloid/microglia/mast-cell/DC/ILC3 genes (`Csf3`, `Cxcl2`, `Il23a`, `Acod1`, `Il10`, `Tnfsf4`, `Tnfsf8`, `Zfp658`, `Glrp1`, `Utf1`, `Peg12`, `Cpvl`, `Dppa2`, `Qrfp`, `Otos`) to `Unsafe`—and completes mapped-universe zero-count rows on pre-filtered GEO reproductive matrices (`Sbp`, `Pbsn`, `Tex19.1`, `Plac1`, `Zfp1010 → CTA-M`; `Serpinb9e`, `Pramel13 → CTA-E`; `Gzmg`, `Gzmd`, `Gzmf → CTA-F`; `Hmga2`, `Gzme → CTA-MF`; `RA-MF-only` reduced from `207` to `41`).
