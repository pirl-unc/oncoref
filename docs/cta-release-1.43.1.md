# OncoRef 1.43.1: whitepaper reorganization and figure updates

OncoRef 1.43.1 is a documentation and figure release. The [1.43.0 curation policy](cta-release-1.43.0.md) (`oncoref.target-selection.2026-11-19.4`), data bundle (`5.23.30`), and antigen memberships are unchanged: **327 CTA, 70 Cancer Antigen only, 128 Reproductive Antigen only, 1,118 Other, and 18,250 Unsafe** across 19,893 human protein groups.

- **Reorganized [antigen catalog whitepaper](antigen-whitepaper.md):** added an Executive Summary with headline counts, consolidated anatomical scope, injury tiers, adult thymus policy, and host protection into one section, moved the `CTAG2` and `GFY` analyses into a dedicated Worked Case Studies section, removed unmotivated prose detours, and grouped links into per-section supporting blocks plus a categorized Reference Index.
- **Populated normal-signal evidence for excluded/held studied CTAs:** switched the studied-protein assay lookup to the full normal-peak evidence ledger and surfaced donor rare-cell percentages so all 11 highlighted exclusions and holds report concrete tissue and expression evidence.
- **New curated literature sources figure & table:** added [literature-sources.png](audits/target-selection-20261110/rebuild/summary/literature-sources.png) and [literature-sources.csv](audits/target-selection-20261110/rebuild/summary/literature-sources.csv) breaking down all 149 catalog-mapped curated literature sources by antigen class (*CTA / cancer-germline*, *Oncofetal / DUX4 / embryo*, *Placental / reproductive*, and *Tumor-vasculature / focused reviews*) alongside their OncoRef dispositions and retention rates.
- **Upgraded whitepaper & curation figures:** replaced the duplicated rescaled right panel on the curation funnels (`funnel-all.png`, `funnel-published.png`) with single-panel layouts (broken x-axis only on `funnel-all.png`, unbroken linear axis on `funnel-published.png`) plus a color legend and green surviving-candidate bar; merged the duplicate `hippocampus` row and grouped `tpm-coverage.png` / `shared-coverage.png` by anatomical scope; highlighted held proteins and landmark genes on `tumor-normal.png`; added explicit `CTA = 327` callout on `venn.png`; and polished `cta-tiers-and-profiles.png`.
