# OncoRef 1.43.0

OncoRef 1.43.0 publishes the revised human antigen catalog and its regeneratable evidence whitepaper.

- Use the within-tissue P95 for healthy bulk panels with individual measurements, with donor aggregation where identities are available. Sample warnings and sparse cell measurements remain visible.
- Classify the adult whole thymus as Disposable and report thymic RNA separately from both enrichment denominators. Resolved thymic cell populations retain their own injury tiers.
- Access organ and cell loss evidence, published gene lists, native measurements and source downloads through the antigen data API. Known shared Allen donor IDs count once across linked collections.
- Trace category counts, studied CTAs, peak tumor measurements and normal exclusions in the whitepaper and tissue hierarchy.
