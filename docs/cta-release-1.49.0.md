# Release 1.49.0

Policy `oncoref.target-selection.2026-11-20.2` · Sequence-role policy `oncoref.target-selection-sequence-roles.2026-11-21.1` · Species policy `oncoref.species-screening-policy.2026-11-21.1` · Murine comparison panel `oncoref.mouse-grcm39-tpm.2026-11-22.1`.

Across **19,893** human protein-equivalence groups (**20,099** Ensembl 115 coding loci), the human catalog classifies **327 CTA, 69 Cancer Antigen only, 128 Reproductive Antigen only (524 candidates), 1,120 Other, and 18,249 Unsafe**. Across **21,433** murine protein-equivalence groups (**21,760** Ensembl 115 `GRCm39` coding loci), the murine catalog classifies **20 CTA, 29 Cancer Antigen only, 296 Reproductive Antigen only (345 candidates), 2,593 Other, and 18,495 Unsafe**, while keeping the built PyPI wheel under **20 MB** (`~15.9 MB`).

## Highlights

1. **Unified `reference_data` externalization of ~51 MB of archival and calibration tables (`#738`, `#748`):**
   - Moves heavy historical `v1.19–v1.45` `oncoref.cta` snapshots, CPTAC RNA-protein calibration models, and genome-wide translation-qualification tables out of the PyPI wheel (`pyproject.toml` `exclude-package-data`) onto immutable SHA-256-pinned GitHub release assets (`reference-archival-20261122`), cutting the built PyPI wheel from `~58 MB` to **`~15.9 MB`**.
   - Adds `reference_data.bundled_or_managed_path(...)` and `EXTERNALIZED_ORIGINATED` in `oncoref.data_manifest` so source checkouts resolve local `oncoref/data/` files offline with SHA-256 verification while wheel installs fetch, cache (`ONCOREF_CACHE_DIR` / `CANCERDATA_DATA_DIR`), verify, and prune externalized tables uniformly through `oncoref.catalog`, `oncoref data`, and `oncoref.cache_retention`.

2. **HPA v23 normal-library biopsy adjudications & Ensembl 115 rare-cell attribution (`#711`, `#712`, `#749`):**
   - Adds `oncoref.normal_adjudications` (`normal_library_peak_adjudications`, `hpa_normal_statistic_sensitivity`, `rare_cell_candidate_adjudications`, `adjudicated_rare_cell_candidate_entity_ids`) backed by three curated tables (`normal-library-peak-adjudications.csv`, `hpa-normal-statistic-sensitivity.csv`, `rare-cell-candidate-adjudications.csv`) and [Normal library & cell attribution audit](audits/normal-attribution-20261122/index.md).
   - Evaluates biopsy-level tissue-marker signatures, GTEx v11 / HPA v25.1 cross-atlas concordance, paralog 31-mer k-mer ambiguity, and genome-wide sensitivity across HPA v23 biopsy summary statistics (`max`, `second_max`, `p95`, `median`, `trimmed_max`), alongside Ensembl 115 host-exon geometry and `Unsafe` background propagation across all 15 tenfold `Other:Review` rare-cell candidates.

3. **`THYMCA` WHO thymic carcinoma cohort & linear GRCh38.p13 TPM `GSE125285` `BCC` / `cSCC` upgrades (`#737`, `#750`):**
   - Splits `TCGA-THYM` by primary GDC WHO histology (`ICDO-3 8586/3` vs `8580–8585`) into disjoint **`THYM`** (`n = 108` thymoma) and **`THYMCA`** (`n = 11` thymic carcinoma) under the **`THYM_EPITHELIAL`** (`n = 119`) umbrella (`docs/audits/thym-histology.md`).
   - Replaces legacy `log2`-derived `GSE125285` `BCC` (`n = 25`) and `cSCC` (`n = 10`) matrices with linear `TPM` computed directly from NCBI GEO `GRCh38.p13` raw counts and GTF union exon lengths (`source-v5.22.15`, data bundle `v5.23.31`), advancing both cohorts to `source_scale_class = "linear_rnaseq_tpm"`.

4. **Quantitative adult human normal panels across unmeasured home tissues (`#735`, `#751`):**
   - Adds `oncoref.unmeasured_home_panels` (`unmeasured_home_tissue_panels`, `unmeasured_home_protein_adjudications`, `unmeasured_home_gene_expression`, `unmeasured_home_tissue_summary`) and [Unmeasured home-tissue panels audit](audits/unmeasured-home-panels-20261122/index.md), externalizing genome-wide expression on `reference-unmeasured-homes-20261122`.
   - Integrates **11** SHA-256-pinned adult human bulk `TPM` (`GSE80249` olfactory mucosa, `GSE221229` fungiform taste papillae, `GSE305125` dental pulp, `GSE344036` mandible bone), donor-resolved 10x/Drop-seq `UMI` (`GSE139522` olfactory neuroepithelium, `CELLxGENE` adult nasal mucosa, `GSE129611` + `GSE193269` scalp hair follicle, `GSE164157` molar dental pulp, `GSE147390` primary osteoblasts), and TaqMan copy-number (`Verbeurgt 2014`) panels without cross-scale conversion, adjudicating all **568** `home_tissue` protein groups (**95** `CA`/`CTA`-gated).

5. **Genome-wide quantitative Ensembl 115 `GRCm39` murine antigen catalog (`#734`, `#752`):**
   - Evaluates all **21,433** primary-assembly `Mus musculus` (`taxon 10090`, `GRCm39` / `GCA_000001635.9`) protein-equivalence groups (**21,760** coding loci, **66,037** qualified translations) across **8/8** active pillars (`Bgee 15.2` + `GSE219045` somatic `TPM`, **16/16** reproductive/accessory/bicornuate/mammary/embryo compartments including `coagulating_gland`, `seminal vesicle`, `uterine_horn`, and `lactating_mammary`, `Tabula Muris` 10x droplet `UMI`, `MGI GXD 6.27` adult `TS28` IHC/Immunoblot with zero negative-IHC ceiling multipliers, 31-mer cDNA paralog risk, and **8** strain-explicit syngeneic/GEMM tumor cohorts [`MC38`, `B16F10`, `CT26`, `LLC`, `4T1`, `KP_LUAD`, `BMPC_PRAD`, `P815`] across `C57BL/6`, `BALB/c`, `FVB`, and `DBA/2`), externalized on `reference-mouse-20261122`.
   - Exposes `mouse.target_selection()`, `mouse.sex_restricted_antigens()`, `mouse.host_exposure()`, `mouse.screening_sources()`, `mouse.tumor_cohorts()`, `antigen_sets.select(..., species="mouse")`, offline `antigen_sets.sequences(..., species="mouse")` + `PeptideIndex` self-proteome subtraction, `oncoref antigens --species mouse`, [Multi-species antigen whitepaper](species-antigen-whitepaper.md), and [Murine antigen catalog audit](audits/mouse-target-catalog-20261122/index.md).
