## 4. Historical Benchmark Disposition: `P1A` (`Trap1a`, `ENSMUSG00000051257`)

`P1A` (`Trap1a`, `PEG:4ab95201a7545cb6364b`, `ENSMUSP00000059448.4`, 224 aa) is the foundational murine tumor rejection antigen cloned from `DBA/2` `P815` mastocytoma (`M36387.1` / `AAA40475.1`, Van den Eynde et al. 1991, sequence-identical to `C57BL/6J` `ENSMUSP00000059448.4`). In the quantitative catalog:

- **Reproductive & developmental expression:** `1,023.45 TPM` in `placenta`, `93.62 TPM` in `embryo`, and `24.41 TPM` in `testis`.
- **Somatic counterevidence hold (`Other:Review`):** Curated somatic RT-PCR/Southern positivity in `BALB/c` lung, spleen, and liver (`sarma1999`, PMID:10049945) holds `Trap1a` in `Other:Review` rather than admitting it to `CTA`, while `mouse.target_selection("P1A")` and `oncoref antigens explain P1A --species mouse` resolve the historical `P1A` alias directly to `ENSMUSG00000051257`.
