standard

  • Off target: the highest tissue value outside tumors and reproductive tissue, Disposable and any protected host compartments included, is below 5 TPM.

  • Sets: keep proteins that are then in either antigen set.

strict

  • Off target: the highest tissue value outside tumors and reproductive tissue, Disposable and any protected host compartments included, is below 4 TPM.
  • Curation: no recorded normal home in a Vital or Protected tissue.
  • Sets: keep proteins that are then in cta.
  • Papers: at least 3 distinct paper(s) nominating it or reporting it in tumors.
  • Exclusions: drop labels starting with MAGE, except MAGEA4.

4. Curation: one set of records for catalog and filters

The catalog and the filters read the same literature review and the same tissue-tier registry. A gene-specific report in normal adult Vital or Protected tissue, with a quote retrieved from the source, makes a protein Unsafe in the catalog. A confirmed finding that falls short of that, a family rule by name or the nose and skin reference is recorded with its tier. When that home is a tissue no panel resolves and no reproductive evidence qualifies, the catalog makes the protein Other:Unscreened tissue; otherwise only the filters act on the tier.
