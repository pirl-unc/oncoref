# Release 1.48.0

Policy `oncoref.target-selection.2026-11-20.2` · Sequence-role policy `oncoref.target-selection-sequence-roles.2026-11-21.1` · Near-self subtraction contract `oncoref.near-self-subtraction.2026-11-21.1` · Isoform-selectivity contract `oncoref.isoform-selectivity.2026-11-22.1` · ncORF/TE contract `oncoref.ncorf-te-screening.2026-11-22.1` · Species policy `oncoref.species-screening-policy.2026-11-21.1`.

Across **19,893** human protein-equivalence groups (**20,099** Ensembl 115 coding loci), the catalog classifies **327 CTA, 69 Cancer Antigen only, 128 Reproductive Antigen only (524 candidates), 1,120 Other, and 18,249 Unsafe**.

## Highlights

1. **Controlled-access local FASTQ + Salmon builder and `unc-nutm1` provenance scaffolding (`#665`, `#604`, `#743`):**
   - Adds `LocalFastqLane`, `LocalFastqSample`, `LocalFastqSalmonSource`, `load_local_fastq_manifest`, `verify_local_fastq_sample_reads`, `quantify_local_fastq_salmon_sample`, and `build_local_fastq_salmon_source_matrices` in `oncoref.expression_builders`, plus `scripts/build_local_fastq_salmon_source.py`.
   - Shares the Ensembl 115 RNA + ncRNA Salmon transcriptome (`cb3481247fad636b3043fd585788f90bea5825c3f01805f333b103f2a56b3540`) and `--validateMappings --gcBias --seqBias` quantification pipeline with `SraSalmonSource`, supports multi-lane biological libraries, excludes matched-normal / control libraries from tumor matrices, and strips all local filesystem paths from provenance manifests.
   - Wires `unc-nutm1` in `expression_sources.yaml` (`expected_samples_by_code: {NUTM: 3}`, `access_level: controlled`, `acquisition_status: local_manifest_required`), reducing `external_build_exemption` rows across `source_matrix_regeneration_audit()` to **0** while retaining `source_scale_class: invalid_legacy_tpm` on legacy `NUTM` (`source-v5.22.10`) until the controlled-access case series is regenerated.

2. **`TCGA-TGCT` `TCGA-2G-AAFL-01` cell-of-origin vs benign parenchymal admixture audit (`#713`, `#744`):**
   - Adds `oncoref.tgct_localization` (`tgct_reproductive_localization_audit`, `tgct_aafl_protein_support`, `tgct_admixture_sole_witness_entity_ids`, `tgct_admixture_restricted_entity_ids`) backed by `tgct-reproductive-localization-audit.csv` (all 154 `TCGA-TGCT` libraries) and `tgct-aafl-protein-support.csv` (all 34 `AAFL`-peaking catalog proteins scanned across all 132 QC-passing curation tumor matrices).
   - Demonstrates via somatic Leydig (`INSL3 = 157.1 TPM`, `CYP17A1 = 23.2 TPM` [166× non-`R` median], `STAR = 66.1 TPM`) + somatic Sertoli (`CLU = 2,019.4 TPM`, `INHA = 45.6 TPM` [43×], `SOX9 = 10.8 TPM`, `WT1 = 9.8 TPM`) niche markers and spermatid stoichiometry (`PRM1 = 1,706.9 TPM` [7.20% GTEx Testis], `PRM2 = 1,362.3 TPM` [5.90% GTEx Testis], log10 stoichiometry `r = 0.9875`) that `TCGA-2G-AAFL-01` is an embryonal-carcinoma-dominant specimen (`POU5F1 = 1,404.8 TPM`) containing `~5–7%` admixed benign testicular parenchyma.
   - Stratifies the 22 `AAFL`-peaking CTAs into **9** independently confirmed outside `R`-flagged `TCGA-TGCT` (`HMGB4`, `ACTL7A`, `LELP1`, `IRGC`, `SMCP`, `TEX38`, `ODF1`, `ACTRT2`, `CIMIP4`), **2** restricted to `R`-flagged `TCGA-TGCT` libraries (`PRM1`, `PRM2`), and **11** `TCGA-2G-AAFL-01` sole-witness admixture candidates (`BOD1L2`, `CETN1`, `CCDC70`, `TSSK2`, `PGK2`, `PRR30`, `ZNRF4`, `SPATA19`, `IQCF5`, `BPIFA3`, `CYLC2`).

3. **Umbrella expression coverage, non-comparable proxy-scale cohort disclosures, and package-data retention audit (`#737`, `#738`, `#745`):**
   - Extends `cancer_reference_expression_availability(..., include_aggregates=True)` and adds `umbrella_expression_coverage()` and `non_comparable_expression_cohorts(include_invalid=True)` in `oncoref.expression`, exposing member completeness (`complete`, `partial_member_gap`, `parent_fallback_gap`) and proxy-scale / withheld cohort flags (`NUTM` + 10 proxy-scale cohorts including `SARC_MYXLPS` and `PANET`).
   - Adds `package_data_size_audit()` and `package_data_size_summary()` in `oncoref.data_manifest`, classifying every packaged file under `oncoref/data/` into `core_runtime` (`< 20 MB`), `active_calibration_or_audit`, and `archival_compatibility_snapshot`.

4. **Germline polymorphism subtraction, isoform-selectivity evaluation, and ncORF / TE screening contract (`#736`, `#746`):**
   - Adds `CodingVariant`, `apply_coding_variants`, and `PeptideIndex.with_coding_variants` in `oncoref.peptide_subtraction` to expand self-subtraction backgrounds with common population missense polymorphisms (`AF >= min_af`, default `0.001` = `0.1%`), failing closed on 1-based reference amino-acid mismatches and preserving variant provenance (`variant_id`, `variant_af`, `variant_source_id`).
   - Adds `ISOFORM_SELECTIVITY_CONTRACT_ID`, `isoform_selectivity_contract()`, and `evaluate_isoform_selectivity(...)` in `oncoref.antigen_sets`, enforcing that locus-level `Unsafe` precedence never yields unless healthy tissues are quantified at isoform/junction resolution (`normal_isoform_tpm <= max_normal_isoform_tpm`) and candidate-unique peptides survive `Unsafe` subtraction (`retained_unique_kmers > 0`).
   - Adds `NCORF_TE_CONTRACT_ID` and `ncorf_te_screening_contract()` in `oncoref.sequence_bundle` formalizing empirical Ribo-seq / immunopeptidomics translation evidence, multicopy TE mappability, and donor-resolved normal-atlas gating.
