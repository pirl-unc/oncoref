# Release 1.53.0

Policy `oncoref.target-selection.2026-11-26.2` · Comparison panel `oncoref.human-grch38-integrated-tpm.2026-11-26.2` · Sequence-role policy `oncoref.target-selection-sequence-roles.2026-11-21.1` · Species policy `oncoref.species-screening-policy.2026-11-21.1` · Canine comparison panel `oncoref.dog-canfam31-tpm.2026-11-26.2` · Murine comparison panel `oncoref.mouse-grcm39-tpm.2026-11-26.2`.

OncoRef `1.53.0` harmonizes target discovery holistically across **human (`9606`)**, **domestic dog (`9615`)**, and **laboratory mouse (`10090`)**—bringing **all `8/8` screening pillars live in every species**, separating *in vivo* tumor witnesses from pure *in vitro* cancer cell-line purity/model-selection sidecars (`cell_line_max_tpm`, `cell_line_top_model`, `qualifying_cell_lines`), annotating ectopic derepression versus homologous lineage retention (`tumor_activation_mode`), enforcing a uniform cross-species monogenic chemosensory GPCR guard (`OR*`/`COR*`/`Olfr*`, `TAAR*`/`Taar*`, `TAS1R*`, `TAS2R*`, `VN1R*`/`Vmn*r*` → `0 CA / 0 CTA`), and restructuring both whitepapers ([`antigen-whitepaper.md`](antigen-whitepaper.md) and [`species-antigen-whitepaper.md`](species-antigen-whitepaper.md)) into self-contained research papers with conceptual process and comparative results figures:

- **Human (`Homo sapiens`, `9606`, `19,893` PEGs / `20,099` Ensembl 115 coding loci, `8/8` pillars active):** **322 CTA** (`222 CTA-M`, `48 CTA-P`, `12 CTA-F`, `40 CTA-MF`; **311/322 = 96.6%** with ectopic activation [`214` pure `ectopic` + `97` `ectopic_and_lineage_retained`], `11` `lineage_retained`), **66 Cancer Antigen only**, **146 Reproductive Antigen only** (`468` total `RA`; `534` candidate union `CA ∪ RA`), **1,110 Other** (`731 Review`, `285 Expression`, `48 Sequence`, `46 Unscreened tissue`), and **18,249 Unsafe**.
- **Dog (`Canis lupus familiaris`, `9615`, `20,082` PEGs / `20,199` Ensembl 98 `CanFam3.1` coding loci, `8/8` pillars active, `reference-dog-20261126-2`):** **118 CTA** (`100 CTA-M`, `7 CTA-P`, `1 CTA-F` [`BMP15`], `10 CTA-MF`; **114/118 = 96.6%** ectopic [`112` `ectopic` + `2` `ectopic_and_lineage_retained`], `4` `lineage_retained`), **127 Cancer Antigen only**, **313 Reproductive Antigen only** (`431` total `RA`; `558` candidate union), **2,201 Other**, and **17,323 Unsafe** across **19** spontaneous companion-dog patient tumor cohorts (`935` primary patient samples) + **12** pure canine cancer cell lines (`canine.cell_lines()`).
- **Mouse (`Mus musculus`, `10090`, `21,433` PEGs / `21,760` Ensembl 115 `GRCm39` coding loci, `8/8` pillars active, `reference-mouse-20261126-2`):** **8 CTA** (`5 CTA-M`, `2 CTA-P`, `1 CTA-MF`, `0 CTA-F`; **6/8 = 75.0%** ectopic [`Hmga2`, `Tex19.1`, `Wfdc13`, `Serpinb9e`, `Pramel13`, `Plac1`] + `2` `lineage_retained` [`Sbp`, `Pbsn` in `BMPC_PRAD`]), **30 Cancer Antigen only**, **303 Reproductive Antigen only** (`311` total `RA`; `341` candidate union), **2,514 Other**, and **18,578 Unsafe** across **14** *in vivo* syngeneic, orthotopic, `GEMM`, and carcinogen tumor cohorts (`57` *in vivo* samples) + **9** pure *in vitro* murine cancer cell lines (`mouse.cell_lines()`).

## Highlights

1. **Preserving `CTA = CA ∩ RA` with explicit `tumor_activation_mode` (`"ectopic"` | `"ectopic_and_lineage_retained"` | `"lineage_retained"`):**
   - Classifies every qualifying `CA`/`CTA` across human, dog, and mouse against `HOMOLOGOUS_CANCER_CODES_BY_COMPARTMENT` (`oncoref.species_target_selection.classify_tumor_activation_mode`), retaining homologous-organ witnesses (`SEMG2` in human `PRAD`, `CSN2`/`CSN3` in canine `CMT`, `Pbsn`/`Sbp` in murine `BMPC_PRAD`, `PRDM14`/`KHDC3L` in human `TGCT`) for `HostContext` (`Accept loss` / `Absent`) while letting callers filter directly on ectopic activation (`311/322` human, `114/118` canine, `6/8` murine CTAs).

2. ***In vivo* tumor requirement for `CA`/`CTA` + pure *in vitro* cell-line purity & model-selection sidecar (`cell_lines()`):**
   - Moves pure *in vitro* cell lines (`CLBL-1` in dog; `4T1` & `P815` in mouse) out of *in vivo* `CA` admission into `comparison_cell_lines` (`canine.cell_lines()` across `12` lines; `mouse.cell_lines()` across `9` lines), populating `cell_line_max_tpm`, `cell_line_top_model`, and `qualifying_cell_lines` without ever granting `CA` or `CTA` on *in vitro* drift alone.
   - Pairing *in vivo* syngeneic grafts with pure cultured lines and expanded splenic/`IL-15`/`IL-2`/`LAK` NK immune panels (`GSE287306`, `GSE336796`, `GSE302070`) cleanly exposes and excludes murine host `TIL` NK granzymes (`Gzmd`, `Gzme`, `Gzmf`, `Gzmg`: `443–2,138 TPM` in *in vivo* grafts vs. `0.05–2.26 TPM` in pure tumor lines → `Unsafe`).

3. **Cross-species monogenic chemosensory GPCR harmonization (`include_chemosensory_gpcr_guard=True`):**
   - Marks all non-`Unsafe` monogenic olfactory, trace-amine, taste, and vomeronasal GPCRs (`OR*`/`COR*`/`Olfr*`, `TAAR*`/`Taar*`, `TAS1R*`, `TAS2R*`, `VN1R*`/`Vmn*r*`) as `home_tissue_unmeasured = True` across human, dog, and mouse—moving `29` human `CA only` GPCRs (`OR8K5`, `TAS1R2`, `OR7C2`, `OR13C9`, `OR2D2`, etc.) to `Other:Unscreened tissue` and `3` human `CTA` ORs (`OR1N1 → RA-F-only`, `OR4M2 → RA-M-only`, `OR10J1 → RA-M-only`), leaving `0` unvalidated chemosensory GPCRs in `CA`/`CTA` in every species while preserving `include_chemosensory_gpcr_guard=False` single-rule ablation parity (`325` CTA).

4. **All `8/8` screening pillars active in dog (`GSE225599` healthy PBMC 10x scRNA-seq) & `19` spontaneous canine tumor cohorts (`935` patients):**
   - Closes `canine_normal_scrna_gap` (`7/8 → 8/8` active pillars) using donor-resolved healthy companion-dog circulating leukocyte 10x scRNA-seq (`GSE225599`, `7` healthy dogs `H_1..H_7`, `150,155` donor × cell-type UMI evaluations) plus healthy companion-dog somatic/alveolar-macrophage controls (`GSE283373`, `GSE288068`, `GSE196108`, `GSE317244`, `GSE345838`), activated-TIL checkpoint/cytokine gates (`PDCD1`, `TNFRSF4`, `IFNG`, etc.), and genome-wide `Other:Review` holds on unannotated `CanFam3.1` single-exon `>=50%` `Unsafe`-paralog retrocopies.
   - Expands spontaneous companion-dog patient cohorts from `7` to **19 cohorts (`935` primary tumors)** across osteosarcoma, mammary carcinoma, hemangiosarcoma, multicentric lymphoma (`LYM`, `LYM_CHOP`), soft-tissue sarcoma (`STS`, `STS_SUB`), invasive urothelial carcinoma (`URO`), oral SCC (`COSCC`), ameloblastoma (`CAA`), histiocytic sarcoma (`HS`), glioma (`GLIOMA`, `GLIOMA_G23`), meningioma (`MNG`), adrenocortical (`ACC`) & thyroid (`THCA`) carcinoma, and acute leukemia (`LEUK`)—validating both **`PRAME` (`CTA-M`)** and **`SSX6` (`CTA-M`)** among the 14 literature RT-PCR nominations and expanding canine CTAs from `54` to **`118`**.
