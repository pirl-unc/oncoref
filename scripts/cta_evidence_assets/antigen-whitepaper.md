## Abstract

Selecting endogenous self-encoded proteins for T-cell receptor therapies (`TCR-T`, soluble `ImmTAC` bispecifics), cancer vaccines, and antibody–drug conjugates requires answering a coupled safety-and-specificity question: *which coding proteins reach high abundance in primary tumors while remaining silent across every vital and protected adult somatic cell population?* Historical cancer–testis antigen (`CTA`) databases conflate legacy RT-PCR and microarray nominations with heterogeneous expression scales, omit single-cell and activated-immune blind spots, and bake patient-specific anatomy (such as male testis or maternal placenta) directly into biological gene labels.

OncoRef formulates target discovery from first principles across three mammalian species—**human** (*Homo sapiens*, **$n** protein-equivalence groups across **$loci** Ensembl 115 coding loci), **domestic dog** (*Canis lupus familiaris*, **$dog_n** groups across **$dog_loci** `CanFam3.1` loci), and **laboratory mouse** (*Mus musculus*, **$mouse_n** groups across **$mouse_loci** `GRCm39` loci)—using one species-invariant mathematical rule set parameterized natively per species across all **8/8** canonical screening pillars (`$policy`). Calibrated against **$tcr_gold_n** human clinical `TCR-T` and `ImmTAC` benchmark regimens, OncoRef flags **$tcr_toxic_caught_n/$tcr_toxic_n (`100%`)** of severe human clinical specificity toxicities as <span class="cat cat-unsafe">Unsafe</span> while retaining **$tcr_safe_cta_kept_n/$tcr_safe_cta_n (`100%`)** of potency-proven clinically safe CTAs. Across the human proteome, multi-modal somatic screening excludes **$unsafe_n** <span class="cat cat-unsafe">Unsafe</span> and holds **$other_n** <span class="cat cat-other">Other</span> protein groups, yielding **$cta** <span class="cat cat-cta">CTA</span> (`$cta_ectopic_any_n/$cta = $cta_ectopic_any_pct%` with ectopic activation outside homologous source organs), **$ca_only** <span class="cat cat-ca">Cancer Antigen only</span>, and **$ra_only** <span class="cat cat-ra">Reproductive Antigen only</span> (**$union** total candidates), partitioned downstream into four mutually exclusive host-physiology classes (`$cta_m_n` `CTA-M`, `$cta_p_n` `CTA-P`, `$cta_f_n` `CTA-F`, `$cta_mf_n` `CTA-MF`).

## Goals and high-level conceptual architecture

OncoRef's architecture is designed around four foundational principles that separate somatic hazard, technical purity, biological expression identity, and patient-specific clinical context:

1. **Strict precedence of multi-modal somatic safety (`Stage 1`) over tumor expression.** No single assay captures every normal-tissue liability: bulk RNA misses `1%–5%` rare parenchymal cells (such as alveolar type 2 pneumocytes, cortical neurons, or ocular melanocytes), resting whole blood misses cytokine-activated T/NK/macrophage effectors, droplet `snRNA-seq` introduces nuclear pre-mRNA readthrough, and mRNA does not always track stable protein. Every dataset is evaluated in its own native scale (`TPM`, `nTPM`, `CP10K`, donor-replicated `UMI`, immunohistochemistry, or quantitative `TMT-iBAQ`) against explicit adult tissue-injury ceilings (`Vital ≤ 4`, `Protected ≤ 8`, `Manageable > 128` → <span class="cat cat-unsafe">Unsafe</span>). Confirmed Vital or Protected adult somatic expression permanently excludes a protein regardless of how strongly a tumor expresses it.
2. **Explicit technical-purity, sequence-containment, and unscreened-niche guards (`Stage 2`).** Proteins that clear measured somatic panels can still mislead target selection through three failure modes: *(i)* `≥95%` 9-mer containment in an <span class="cat cat-unsafe">Unsafe</span> protein or readthrough translation (`Other:Sequence`); *(ii)* unmeasured adult anatomical micro-niches—including monogenic chemosensory GPCRs (`OR*`, `TAS1R*`, `TAS2R*`, `VN1R*`) expressed at high per-cell abundance inside `~0.1%` singular olfactory sensory neurons or taste/solitary chemosensory cells diluted `1,000×` in bulk mucosa (`Other:Unscreened tissue`); and *(iii)* genomic-DNA contamination (`>2.5%` ectopic olfactory receptors `≥5 TPM`) or host tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (`TIL`) RNA in bulk tumor biopsies. OncoRef therefore requires *in vivo* patient or graft tumor witnesses for <span class="cat cat-ca">CA</span> admission while evaluating pure *in vitro* cancer cell lines separately as an experimental model reference (`cell_line_max_tpm`, `qualifying_cell_lines`).
3. **Orthogonal biological identity (`CA`, `RA`, `CTA`) with explicit ectopic vs. lineage-retained provenance (`Stage 3`).** Against one shared non-thymic, non-reproductive healthy adult baseline **H₀ = max(Normal N, Disposable D, $floor TPM)**, <span class="cat cat-ca">Cancer Antigens (CA)</span> (`≥$fold × H₀` in *in vivo* tumors) and <span class="cat cat-ra">Reproductive Antigens (RA)</span> (`≥$fold × H₀` in adult reproductive organs or supported embryo lineages) are defined symmetrically, with **CTA = CA ∩ RA**. Homologous-organ tumors (`SEMG2` in prostate `PRAD`, `CSN2` in breast `BRCA`, `PRDM14` in `TGCT`) remain genuine biological `CTA`s because they are actionable in patients whose homologous organ is absent or expendable, while `tumor_activation_mode` (`"ectopic"`, `"ectopic_and_lineage_retained"`, `"lineage_retained"`) explicitly distinguishes true somatic derepression from tissue-of-origin retention.
4. **Downstream `HostContext` anatomy and native cross-species parameterization without orthology transfer.** Biological `CA` / `RA` / `CTA` labels never hardcode the sex or reproductive state of a patient; downstream `HostContext` profiles (`Protect`, `Accept loss`, `Absent`) induce a clean 4-way physiological partition (`{CTA-M, CTA-P, CTA-F, CTA-MF}`). Across **human (`9606`)**, **dog (`9615`)**, and **mouse (`10090`)**, the identical mathematical pipeline executes on each species' verified proteome and comparative anatomy with zero cross-species label borrowing.

## Categories

Every protein-equivalence group (`PEG`) in the coding proteome lands in one of five mutually exclusive categories:

- <span class="cat cat-unsafe">Unsafe</span> (**$unsafe_n**): confirmed expression in vital, protected, or high-expression manageable adult somatic tissues or sorted/activated immune populations. Normal-tissue safety always takes precedence over tumor expression.
- <span class="cat cat-other">Other</span> (**$other_n**): kept out of the candidate sets without being confirmed <span class="cat cat-unsafe">Unsafe</span>—either because required evidence is incomplete or ambiguous (missing normal-tissue coverage or unresolved paralog attribution in single-cell data `Other:Review`, or `≥95%` 9-mer overlap with <span class="cat cat-unsafe">Unsafe</span> proteins `Other:Sequence`), or because tumor and reproductive expression do not qualify (below the `$fold×` threshold `Other:Expression`, or an unscreened adult somatic micro-niche / monogenic chemosensory GPCR without reproductive enrichment `Other:Unscreened tissue`).
- <span class="cat cat-ca">Cancer Antigens (CA)</span> (**$ca** total; **$ca_only** `CA only`): passes all safety, coverage, sequence, and home-niche screens and reaches **≥$fold × H₀** in at least one QC-passing *in vivo* tumor sample.
- <span class="cat cat-ra">Reproductive Antigens (RA)</span> (**$ra** total; **$ra_only** `RA only`): passes the shared safety and eligibility screens and reaches **≥$fold × H₀** in an adult reproductive tissue sample or cell-supported early-embryo lineage.
- <span class="cat cat-cta">Cancer–Testis Antigens (CTA)</span> (**$cta**): the intersection **CTA = CA ∩ RA**, clearing both *in vivo* cancer and reproductive/developmental enrichment while remaining silent across healthy somatic organs. The candidate union **CA ∪ RA** contains **$union** protein groups (`$cta` <span class="cat cat-cta">CTA</span>, `$ca_only` <span class="cat cat-ca">CA only</span>, `$ra_only` <span class="cat cat-ra">RA only</span>).

## Why bulk RNA alone is insufficient

Relying on bulk tissue RNA medians or P95 summaries alone leaves fatal normal-tissue blind spots. OncoRef layers seven complementary normal-tissue modalities so each assay covers the structural blind spots of the others:

- **Paired bulk RNA atlases and sorted/activated immune panels.** Bulk RNA removes ubiquitously expressed housekeeping genes, but individual atlases differ in organ sampling and postmortem artifacts. Pairing deconvolved GTEx v11 donor P95s (after stoichiometric multiplex-bleed removal) with HPA v23 biopsies and brain subregions prevents single-atlas gaps from reading as silence, while sorted and stimulated leukocyte subsets (`HPA`, `DICE`, `Monaco` in human; `ImmGen`, `LPS`/`IL-4` BMDM, `IL-15`/`IL-2` NK and Treg panels in mouse; `GSE225599` scRNA + activated-TIL gates in dog) catch effector transcripts diluted in whole blood.
- **Single-cell clusters and consortium-deduplicated rare-cell UMI atlases.** Bulk RNA averages across millions of cells, diluting a transcript expressed at `50 TPM` inside `1%–3%` of a vital cell population (such as alveolar type 2 pneumocytes, cardiac myocytes, ocular melanocytes, or cortical neurons) below `1 TPM`. Single-cell cluster `nTPM` (`HPA`, adult cornea `GSE155683`) and donor-resolved `UMI` atlases (`CELLxGENE Census` + `Siletti` brain; `Tabula Muris`; `GSE225599`) catch these localized risks. Requiring `≥2` UMIs per cell replicated across independent studies and donors avoids discarding real targets over droplet ambient soup, while intronic nuclear `snRNA-seq` readthrough (`PLAC1`, `DUX4`) is separated from genuine cytoplasmic mRNA (`MAGEA12`).
- **Normal-tissue protein staining (`IHC`) and quantitative proteomics.** Steady-state mRNA does not always mirror protein abundance: long-lived structural proteins persist at modest transcript levels, whereas untranslated chromatin leakage (`LALBA` in spermatids; `PRAME` in adrenal fibroblasts) produces `4–12 TPM` RNA with zero detectable protein. Reliable positive HPA immunohistochemistry (or `>25–50 ppm` canine tissue TMT proteomics / murine `GXD` `TS28` staining) independently marks a protein `Unsafe`, whereas routine negative human IHC provides calibrated headroom (`2×` Approved, `4×` Supported, `8×` Enhanced) above low bulk RNA.
- **Specialized adult home-tissue panels and monogenic chemosensory GPCR guards.** Standard multi-organ atlases omit mineralized bone, dental pulp, hair cortex, olfactory sensory neurons, and taste buds. Integrating SHA-256-pinned adult bone and olfactory panels excludes ceiling-violating proteins (`SP7` `193 TPM` in mandible bone; `GFY` in `94.1%` of mature olfactory neurons) as `Unsafe`, while holding unscreened micro-niches (`DSPP`) and monogenic chemosensory GPCRs (`OR*`, `TAS1R*`, `TAS2R*`, `VN1R*`) out of `CA`/`CTA`.

## Clinical toxicity gold standard: potency-proven TCR-T and TCR-bispecific trials

Healthy-tissue bulk `TPM`, single-cell `nTPM`, immunohistochemistry, donor-resolved rare-cell `UMI` counts, and `1-mismatch` peptide-overlap screens are **mechanistic proxies**—not direct measurements of human T-cell toxicity. The empirical gold standard for intracellular target safety is **human clinical experience with peptide–MHC-directed T-cell receptors (`TCR-T` and soluble `ImmTAC` bispecifics) targeting endogenous self-encoded proteins in trials that achieved objective anti-tumor responses (`ORR > 0%`)**. Requiring confirmed clinical tumor regression (or fulminant *in vivo* target-cell cytolysis) is essential: when a trial sees `0% ORR` (`IMA202` against `MAGEA1`, `0/16` responses), the absence of healthy-organ injury cannot prove the target tissue is antigen-negative, because receptor avidity, cell dose, or engraftment may simply have been insufficient to lyse antigen-bearing cells (`oncoref.tcr_trials.gold_standard`, `oncoref tcr-trials --table benchmark`).

Across **$tcr_gold_n** curated endogenous-protein clinical `TCR-T` / `ImmTAC` benchmark entries (**$tcr_potent_n** potency-proven regimens plus **$tcr_nonpotent_n** segregated `0% ORR` or unreadout controls; somatic neoantigens such as `KRAS G12D` and viral antigens such as `HPV16 E6/E7` are excluded because they lack a germline normal-proteome counterpart), OncoRef's `1.0×` gate ceiling cleanly separates clinically safe CTAs from lethal or dose-limiting specificity toxicities ([download full benchmark CSV](antigen-whitepaper/clinical-tcr-gold-standard.csv)):

- **`100%` sensitivity to severe human clinical specificity toxicities (`$tcr_toxic_caught_n/$tcr_toxic_n` flagged `Unsafe`, `0` false-safe misses):**
  - *On-target / off-tumor healthy-organ destruction (`4/4` `Unsafe`):* **`CEACAM5`** (`33%` ORR → `3/3` dose-limiting Grade 3 pan-colitis; `563×` colon enterocyte ceiling), **`MLANA` / MART-1** (`DMF5`, `30%` ORR → `70%` rash/vitiligo, `55%` anterior ocular uveitis, `50%` stria vascularis sensorineural hearing loss; `451×` ocular melanocyte `Vital` ceiling), **`PMEL` / gp100** (`19%` TCR-T ORR → `94%` rash, `25%` uveitis, `31%` hearing loss; soluble `tebentafusp` FDA-approved with `>80%` rash but minimal uveitis/ototoxicity across the blood–ocular/labyrinthine barrier; `889×` ocular melanocyte ceiling), and **`TYR` / tyrosinase** (`TIL1383I`, `33%` ORR → diffuse vitiligo; `75×` ocular melanocyte ceiling). Together, `MLANA`, `PMEL`, and `TYR` directly validate why OncoRef separates `Disposable` cutaneous melanocytes from `Vital` ocular and inner-ear pigment cells.
  - *Fatal paralog and structural off-target cross-reactivity (`2/2` `Unsafe`):* **`MAGEA3` (`HLA-A*02:01 / KVAELVHFL`, `56%` ORR → `2/9` fatal necrotizing leukoencephalopathy)** is missed in `MAGEA12` bulk brain RNA (`0.5 nTPM`, `0.13×`) but caught on primary `MAGEA3` colon `scRNA-seq` (`13.3×`), qPCR-corroborated rare-cell brain RNA on `MAGEA12` (`4.75%` ≥1-UMI, `0.40%` ≥2-UMI), `EPS8L2` (`46.1×`), and `1-mismatch` peptide overlap (`KVAELVHFL ↔ KMAELVHFL`). **`MAGEA3` (`HLA-A*01:01 / EVDPIGHLY` affinity-enhanced `a3a` TCR → `2/2` fatal cardiogenic shock within 4–5 days)** cross-recognized cardiac **`TTN`** (`ESDPIVAQY`, `53,894 nTPM` cardiomyocytes = `13,473×` ceiling; primary `MAGEA3` itself is also `Unsafe` at `13.3×`); because `4` of `9` residues differ, `a3a` also marks the boundary where sequence k-mers end and experimental X-scan / iPSC-cardiomyocyte screening is required for affinity-matured receptors.
- **`100%` recall on potency-proven clinically safe CTAs (`$tcr_safe_cta_kept_n/$tcr_safe_cta_n` retained as `CTA`):** **`NY-ESO-1`** (`58%` ORR in Robbins 2015, `30%–80%` across MRCLS and myeloma, `0.06×`), **`MAGEA4`** (FDA-approved `afami-cel`, `37%` Phase 2 ORR, `0.01×`), **`PRAME`** (`IMA203`, `52.5%` u/cORR [`28.9%` overall / `54.5%` Ph1b cORR] across `40` patients without nephrotoxicity or adrenal insufficiency, `0.75×`), **`CTAG2` (`LAGE-1a`)** (`lete-cel`, `30%` pooled / `40%` standard-LDR MRCLS and `80%` myeloma ORR without pulmonary or urothelial toxicity, `0.78×`), and **`MAGEA10`** (`ADP-A2M10`, `9.1%` ORR, `0.30×`). Notably, **two of the five clinically proven safe CTAs (`PRAME` and `CTAG2`) require OncoRef's multi-modal calibration**—matched negative-IHC RNA headroom (`PRAME` shifts from `3.0×` naive RNA to `0.75×` under negative IHC) and isoform-scoped vascular perfusion clearance (`CTAG2` `ENST00000369585`)—to avoid false exclusion.
- **Conservative gene-level precision frontier (`$tcr_conservative_n` tolerated non-CTA regimens flagged `Unsafe`):** **`WT1`** (`TTCR-C4`, `100%` post-HCT AML relapse-free survival without podocyte nephrotic syndrome; `9.9×` mesothelial RNA + podocyte IHC `High`), **`MAGEA3` (`HLA-DPB1*04:01` `6F9` CD4 TCR-T, `24%` ORR without CNS/cardiac toxicity; `13.3×` colon `scRNA`)**, and **`AFP`** (`ADP-A2AFP`, `9.5%` HCC ORR without autoimmune hepatitis; `98×` renal proximal tubule `scRNA`) were clinically tolerated for their specific `(peptide, HLA)` complexes—reflecting immunoproteasome-dependent differential processing (`WT1` `RMFPNAPYL`), MHC-II restriction, or sub-threshold parenchymal pMHC density. Gene-level screening intentionally remains `Unsafe` on these proteins because epitope-specific processing escape cannot be generalized across other peptides or surface-targeted modalities.
- **Segregated non-potent or unreadout controls (`$tcr_nonpotent_n` entries excluded from safety calibration):** **`MAGEA1`** (`IMA202`, `0/16 = 0% ORR`) and **`CT83` / KK-LC-1** (ongoing Phase 1 `NCT05035407` / `NCT05483491`) are retained as `CTA-M` solely on independent multi-assay somatic silence (`0.00×` and `0.33×`), never credited as clinically cleared.

## Decision tree, attrition, and the 10× enrichment rule

Every protein group traverses a deterministic decision tree with strict precedence—normal-tissue safety first (`Stage 1`), then coverage, sequence, and home-niche eligibility (`Stage 2`), and finally symmetric **$fold×** enrichment in *in vivo* tumors and reproductive/embryonic compartments (`Stage 3`)—narrowing **$n** protein groups to **$shared** safety- and coverage-eligible proteins and **$union** retained candidates (`CA ∪ RA`).

### Ectopic derepression, cell-line TIL separation, and the chemosensory GPCR guard

Interrogating `CA` and `CTA` across all three mammalian proteomes resolves three fundamental biological questions about how tumor selectivity arises and how technical false positives enter bulk RNA-seq catalogs:

1. **Ectopic somatic derepression vs. homologous lineage retention (`tumor_activation_mode`) — and what `CA only` proteins actually do.** When a reproductive protein reaches `≥10 × H₀` in a tumor of its *own* tissue of origin (`SEMG2` in prostate `PRAD`, `CSN2` in luminal `BRCA`, `PRDM14` and `KHDC3L` in germ-cell `TGCT`, `Pbsn`/`Sbp` in murine `BMPC_PRAD`), the tumor is retaining lineage differentiation rather than ectopically reactivating a silent germline program. OncoRef keeps homologous-organ witnesses inside `CTA`—because a patient who lacks or can accept loss of prostate, resting mammary tissue, or testis can genuinely target those proteins—while recording `tumor_activation_mode` across all qualifying tumor cohorts:
   - Among the **$cta** human CTAs, **$cta_ectopic_any_n (`$cta_ectopic_any_pct%`)** exhibit confirmed **ectopic** activation outside any homologous reproductive/mammary organ (**$cta_ectopic_n** pure `"ectopic"` such as `MAGEA4`, `CT83`, `DUX4`, `PLAC1`, `XAGE1A/B` + **$cta_both_mode_n** `"ectopic_and_lineage_retained"` such as `NY-ESO-1`, `PRAME`, `CTAG2`, `LALBA`, `CSN2`), whereas **$cta_lineage_only_n** ($cta_lineage_only_genes) are pure `"lineage_retained"` (`$dog_cta_ectopic_any_n/$dog_cta` = `$dog_cta_ectopic_any_pct%` ectopic in dog; `$mouse_cta_ectopic_any_n/$mouse_cta` = `$mouse_cta_ectopic_any_pct%` ectopic in mouse).
   - What are the **$ca_only** human <span class="cat cat-ca">Cancer Antigen only</span> proteins (`CA \ RA`)? ## Tissue injury tiers, patient profiles, and sex-restricted CTAs

OncoRef separates three tissue-policy questions: **anatomical role** (healthy baseline vs. <span class="cat cat-ra">RA</span>-qualifying reproductive/embryonic tissue vs. `thymic`), **adult injury tier** (clinical consequence of sustained organ or cell-type loss), and **patient-specific organ protection** (downstream anatomy and tolerance via `--profile`). A tissue can qualify <span class="cat cat-ra">RA</span> while retaining a protective rating—adult testis qualifies <span class="cat cat-ra">RA</span> while carrying a `Protected` injury tier, and breast biopsies are split into lactating versus resting mammary via a coordinated milk-program signature (`LALBA`, `CSN1S1`, `CSN2`, `CSN3`).

| Adult injury tier | Policy interpretation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Vital** | Loss threatens life, essential repair, or major sensory/neurological function | Heart, neurons, corneal endothelium, ocular melanocytes |
| **Protected** | Persistent loss is consequential and clinical compensation is poorly established | Salivary gland, whole skin, adult testis, ovary |
| **Manageable** | Defined loss has an established precedent for clinical compensation or replacement | Thyroid, mature B cells |
| **Disposable** | A narrow compartment carries a specific loss allowance | Cutaneous melanocytes, hair follicles, gallbladder, adult whole thymus |

Bulk measurements inherit the organ tier; permissive cell-type exceptions require resolved cell identity, and mixed populations keep the more protective tier ([organ and cell hierarchy](tissue-hierarchy.md), [tissue tier policy](tissue-tiers.md)). Adult whole thymus is rated `Disposable` under the organ-loss hierarchy, but because medullary thymic epithelial cells (`mTECs`) promiscuously express tissue-restricted antigens during central tolerance ([human mTEC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2211762/)), bulk thymus sits in the `thymic` scope and is excluded from both enrichment denominators (`N` and `D`), while independent normal-expression and protein screens still apply ([thymectomy cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37530823/)).

### Biological membership versus patient-specific filtering

Biological <span class="cat cat-cta">CTA</span>, <span class="cat cat-ca">CA</span>, and <span class="cat cat-ra">RA</span> labels record where a protein is expressed, not what a particular patient can safely lose. Default biological sets leave reproductive tissues unprotected until you opt in. Downstream patient profiles (`female-patient`, `male-patient`, lactation, pregnancy, or custom overrides) mark each compartment as <span class="cat cat-protect">Protect</span>, <span class="cat cat-loss">Accept loss</span>, or <span class="cat cat-absent">Absent</span> ([rules and limits](host-selection.md)). ## Methods: protein grouping, sources, and thresholds

### Protein-group counting unit

Source gene identifiers across RNA, single-cell, and protein atlases map to human **Ensembl 115** loci. Each locus gets one qualified reference translation—preferring **MANE Select** on the primary assembly, then **Ensembl canonical**—checked for coding biotype, complete start/stop codons, uninterrupted ORF, and absence of translation conflicts ([transcript selection audit](audits/cta-transcript-selection-20261108/index.md)).

Paralogous loci encoding identical proteins (`SPANXA1/2`, `XAGE1A/B`, `VCY/1B`) collapse into one protein group by representative amino-acid sequence even when cDNAs differ. Wherever sample identities exist, member-locus expression is summed within each sample before comparing across cohorts. The **$missing_seq** records lacking a qualified representative sequence stay visible; missing translation blocks admission even when RNA is measured ([sequence and peptide-subtraction API](antigen-sequences.md)).

### Evidence sources and native-TPM coverage

OncoRef pins **$files** SHA-256-verified files across **$tissue_rows** source/context rows and **$total_cohorts** QC-passing *in vivo* tumor cohorts (**$baseline_cohorts** baseline cohorts, `$cohorts` in the initial `2026-11-10` audit table, plus `3` upgraded linear-TPM cohorts `THYMCA`, `BCC`, `cSCC`). <summary>Exact quantitative gate thresholds and rare-cell provenance rules</summary>

* **Bulk, cluster, and immune RNA ceilings:** Base limits in TPM and nTPM are **$rna_bases**. Matched negative IHC across routine contexts of the tissue raises the ceiling by 2× (Approved), 4× (Supported), or 8× (Enhanced), alongside limited registry clinical adjustments. Exceeding a `Vital` or `Protected` ceiling establishes `Unsafe`; `Manageable` establishes `Unsafe` only above **$manageable_ceiling**.
* **Donor-resolved rare-cell RNA and consortium provenance:** Evaluated in native UMI counts per adult donor and named cell type. In a `Vital` population, a donor signal requires **≥$cells cells** and **≥$pct%** of cells with ≥2 molecules (replicated across independent study groups), or **≥$frequent%** in a single study; `Protected` populations double those frequency cutoffs. Shared consortium collections (Baylor/Chen eye, Sanes eye, Allen reference brain, Michigan kidney, Teichmann heart) and donors embedded in integrated atlases (HLCA, Baylor/Chen eye, SEA-AD) are resolved to their original study group and biological donor ID before counting replication. Replicated signals without locus mapping risk (or with independent bulk or gene-specific confirmation) establish `Unsafe`; unresolved locus attribution or single-study signals go to `Other:Review`.
* **Structural and home-tissue gates:** Unsupported nested sequences, read-through loci, or ≥95% 9-mer containment in `Unsafe` proteins produce `Other:Sequence`. A curated adult normal tissue outside the native-TPM panels (`DSPP` odontoblasts) or an unvalidated monogenic chemosensory GPCR (`OR*`, `TAS1R*`, `TAS2R*`, `VN1R*`) assigns `Other:Unscreened tissue` when `RA` is negative (or `Reproductive Antigen only` when `RA` qualifies); fetal-only tissues (`AMELX` in developing tooth epithelium) keep their developmental scope.
* **Eligibility and Disposable cap:** Eligibility requires complete coverage across **$required** healthy contexts, target peak above every measured non-reproductive and Disposable value, and Disposable expression ≤ **$cap** TPM and ≤ **$cap** nTPM.
* **Embryo lineage support for RA:** Well-sampled lineages require ≥20 cells, ≥5 cells at ≥1 TPM, and ≥10% positive cells; smaller lineages require ≥5 cells, ≥50% positive cells, and replication in two independent embryos of the same stage and lineage. Within-site donor/biopsy **P95** dampens isolated healthy outliers but cannot diagnose carryover or prove absence from an unmeasured rare cell type.

## Reproducing the catalog and downloads

Every external reference file and per-sample tumor matrix required for a clean rebuild (policy `$policy`) is SHA-256-pinned and fetched through OncoRef's uniform CLI (no out-of-band downloads or machine-local paths):

```sh
# 1. Fetch and verify all pinned reference datasets (add --include-tumors for all 135 tumor cohorts)
python -m oncoref.curation fetch --include-tumors

# 2. Rebuild the full curation graph (catalog, host exposure, figures, reports, whitepaper)
python -m oncoref.curation build

# 3. Verify every tracked implementation file, catalog table, and whitepaper artifact byte-for-byte
python -m oncoref.curation check
python scripts/render_antigen_whitepaper.py --check
```

### Limitations

Cross-cohort tumor matrices come from distinct studies and processing pipelines; OncoRef enforces nonnegative comparable TPM and `≤2.5%` olfactory-receptor gDNA library QC rather than cross-study batch correction. In rare-cell atlases, count matrices cannot separate paralog cross-mapping from genuine sparse transcription without read-level realignment or locus-specific assays (as in `CTAG2`), and while known consortium collections and integrated-atlas donor overlaps are deduplicated, undocumented donor reuse across unrelated public studies cannot be ruled out. Single-sample RNA peaks do not measure intratumoral clonality, translated protein abundance, peptide–MHC presentation, or receptor-specific structural mimicry (such as `MAGEA3` `a3a → TTN`).
